HEVC / H.265 Video Decoder Notice
==================================

The Linux x86_64 build of Fluxer World includes hardware-accelerated HEVC /
H.265 video decoding, provided by a custom Electron build with proprietary
codecs enabled (`proprietary_codecs=true`, `enable_hevc_parser_and_hw_decoder=true`).

This build is sourced from the BranchBit prebuilt Electron release:
  https://github.com/BranchBit/electron-chromium-ffmpeg-hevc-prebuilt

HEVC is a patent-encumbered codec. Distributing software that includes an
HEVC decoder may require licensing from patent pool administrators such as
MPEG LA, Access Advance, and Velos Media. Fluxer World is released under
AGPL-3.0-or-later and the HEVC support is provided "as-is" with no warranty
or claim of licensing on behalf of redistributors.

Linux ARM64, macOS, Windows, Android, and iOS builds do NOT bundle an HEVC
decoder; on those platforms HEVC is decoded via the operating system's
built-in codec where available.

If you redistribute or commercialize a build of Fluxer World that includes
the Linux x86_64 binary, consult appropriate legal counsel about HEVC
patent licensing.
