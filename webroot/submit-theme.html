Submit a theme

Add your theme to the public Fluxer World gallery. Fill the form, hit Submit — it goes live immediately.

The easiest way to get the CSS: open Fluxer's settings → Appearance, customise your theme, click Export as CSS, then paste the contents below. Or use the Load current theme into editor button inside the CSS overrides box.

Log in to publish

You need a fluxer.world account to publish a theme. Log in here and your session sticks for next time.

MFA accounts: log in via fluxer.world/app, then come back.

Pick the 5 colours that best represent your theme — typically bg / surface / hover / accent / text.
