Bug Hunters

Fluxerworld is built by a tiny team — but we have a community that keeps us honest.

What it is

The Bug Hunter badge recognises members who have reported real, reproducible bugs that helped us improve Fluxerworld. It shows up on your profile popout next to your name.

It's not staff. It's not a moderator role. It's just a thank-you that's visible to everyone who hovers your badges.

How to earn it

  • Report a bug that we can reproduce and verify.
  • The more useful the report (screenshots, console output, steps to reproduce), the more likely it sticks.
  • We award badges manually — there's no points system or auto-grant.

The fastest path is to drop into the support guild and ping a maintainer when you find something odd. If you can show it happens consistently, we'll usually fix it the same day.

Where to report

Pick whichever's easiest for you:

Join the support guild   Open a GitHub issue

A note on age

Anyone can earn this badge — there is no age requirement. The badge is purely recognition. Staff and moderator roles do have age and responsibility requirements that the badge doesn't grant.
