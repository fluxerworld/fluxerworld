Fluxerworld Staff & Community Team

A small volunteer team running a community-owned chat platform.

The Team

Fluxerworld is built and run by volunteers. We're a community-owned fork of Fluxer with our own infrastructure, policies, and roadmap.

Staff

The Staff badge marks members who hold administrative responsibilities — running infrastructure, moderating reports, handling abuse, and shipping fixes.

Staff is not a popularity contest. It's a job — with on-call expectations, legal duties under the DSA, and the obligation to enforce the rules consistently.

Community Team

Community Team members help run community channels, welcome new users, and provide front-line support without sitting on Staff. Think of them as the friendly faces in support and announcement rooms.

How to join

We don't have an open application process. Both roles are invitation-only and based on long-term involvement, demonstrated judgement, and (for Staff) the legal capacity to take on responsibility.

  • Staff applicants must be 18+.
  • Community Team has no minimum age but does require an established history in the community.
  • Being helpful, reporting bugs, or moderating your own guild are all great — but they are not paths to staff.

If you're looking for a way to be recognised for your contributions, look at the Bug Hunter badge instead.

Want to support the project?

You don't need a badge to make Fluxerworld better. The biggest help comes from:

  • Reporting reproducible bugs with steps, screenshots, or logs.
  • Inviting friends — small communities matter.
  • Subscribing to Plutonium, which directly funds hosting.
