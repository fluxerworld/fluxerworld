Recognised community guilds that work alongside Fluxerworld.

Partners

A small set of community guilds we work closely with — for moderation collaboration, beta testing, or just because they've helped grow the platform.

What it means

The Partner badge marks members who own or run a guild that's been recognised as a Fluxerworld Partner. It's a small thank-you to community leaders who put in real work building their corner of the platform.

It does not grant any platform privileges. Partners are still subject to the same rules and moderation as everyone else.

How partnerships work

  • We don't have a formal application — partnerships are invitation-based.
  • Partner guilds are typically established communities with a clear theme and consistent moderation.
  • We may approach guild owners when their community has grown organically and aligns with the platform's spirit.

If you're running a guild that's gaining traction and you'd like to chat about formalising a partnership, get in touch.

Talk to us

The fastest path is via the support guild — ping a maintainer in the partnerships channel.
